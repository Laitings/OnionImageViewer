OnionImageViewer uses the following open source libraries.

libheif (HEIF/HEIC reading)
  Copyright (c) struktur AG, Dirk Farin and contributors
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0 (see libheif.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/strukturag/libheif
  Shipped as heif.dll next to OnionImageViewer.exe.

libde265 (HEVC decoding for HEIF/HEIC)
  Copyright (c) struktur AG, Dirk Farin and contributors
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0 (see libde265.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/strukturag/libde265
  Shipped as libde265.dll next to OnionImageViewer.exe.

Both libraries are unmodified builds from vcpkg (https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg, the
versions pinned by vcpkg.json in the OnionImageViewer source). They are dynamically linked:
you may replace heif.dll and libde265.dll with your own builds of a compatible version.

LibRaw (DNG reading)
  Copyright (c) LibRaw LLC and contributors
  License: LGPL 2.1 or CDDL 1.0, with BSD 3-Clause components (see libraw.txt)
  Source: https://github.com/LibRaw/LibRaw
  Shipped as raw_r.dll from vcpkg. You may replace it with a compatible build.
  See jasper.txt, lcms.txt, libjpeg-turbo.txt and zlib.txt for its dependencies.

Qt (user interface)
  Copyright (c) The Qt Company Ltd. and other contributors
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0
  Source: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/
  Shipped as the Qt6*.dll files and plugins next to OnionImageViewer.exe.

OpenEXR and Imath (EXR reading)
  Copyright (c) Contributors to the OpenEXR Project
  License: BSD 3-Clause
  Source: https://github.com/AcademySoftwareFoundation/openexr

libwebp (WebP reading)
  Copyright (c) Google Inc.
  License: BSD 3-Clause (see libwebp.txt)
  Source: https://chromium.googlesource.com/webm/libwebp
  Linked statically into OnionImageViewer.exe.
